    About

    This is my personal vault for the OnePlus Nord 2 5G. I keep the things I have actually flashed and used, with honest notes on what worked and what didn't.

    All ROMs, kernels, recoveries and tools belong to their original developers. Credit and source links are shown beside each file. If you are an author and want something credited differently or removed, open an issue on GitHub.
