    About

    This vault

    This is a personal archive for the OnePlus Nord 2 5G, kept by the owner of a DN2101 running the latest OxygenOS 13. It collects stock firmware, custom ROMs, recoveries, drivers and everyday tools, each with notes on what it is and where it came from.

    The stock firmware, tools and guides come from my own Telegram channel, OPN2-Dump. The custom ROM list was collected from the public OnePlus Nord 2 Updates channel, where ROM maintainers post their builds.

    Known gaps

    The ROM list was read from the public preview of the OnePlus Nord 2 Updates channel. A few older stretches of that channel could not be read, so some early 2021 to 2022 ROM builds may be missing. Each ROM family lists its newest builds first.

    ⚠ Disclaimer

    Flashing custom software can brick your device, void your warranty or cause data loss. Everything here is provided as is, with no warranty. Back up your data and flash at your own risk. Unless a file is tagged Daily driver or Tested, I have not verified it myself.
